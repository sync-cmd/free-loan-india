Privacy Policy

How Freeloan India collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you book an appointment or contact us, we may collect: your name, mobile number, email address (optional), loan type, outstanding amount range, preferred mode of contact, a description of your issue, and — once your case is under representation — loan documents, statements, and communication records you choose to share with us.

2. How we use your information

  • To evaluate your case and respond to your appointment request
  • To provide legal and advisory services, including negotiating with lenders and drafting or filing complaints on your behalf, where engaged to do so
  • To communicate with you about your case via phone, WhatsApp, or email
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations, where applicable

3. How your information reaches us

When you submit the appointment form on this website, your details are transmitted securely to us by email through a third-party form-delivery service (Web3Forms). The service passes the submission on to our email inbox; we do not use it to publish or share your details. We use the details only to contact you about your query.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Your information may be shared only where necessary to represent your case — for example, with the concerned lender or NBFC, or with a regulatory or grievance authority such as the RBI or the RBI Integrated Ombudsman (under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021) — and only to the extent required to pursue your matter. We do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Consent and your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

We process your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) on the basis of your consent, given by ticking the consent box on our form or by contacting us, and for legitimate uses permitted by law. Under the Act you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and how it is used
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to erase your data, subject to any legal duty to retain it
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as it was given (this will not affect work already done)
  • Seek grievance redressal from us and, if unresolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of your death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at the email in Section 11. Our services are meant for adults; we do not knowingly collect personal data of children (under 18 years) without verifiable consent of a parent or guardian.

6. Data security

We take reasonable security safeguards to keep information you share with us confidential. However, no method of electronic communication (including WhatsApp, email, or phone) is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to us.

7. Data retention

We retain your case-related information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet any legal, regulatory, or record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or securely archived.

8. Cookies & website data

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics that collect personal information. The only information collected through the site is what you choose to type into the appointment form and submit.

9. Third-party links

Our website or social media pages may link to third-party sites (such as Instagram or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

11. Contact & grievances

For any privacy-related questions, requests or grievances, reach us at loanfreeeindia@gmail.com or +91 89206 84649. We will respond within a reasonable time.
