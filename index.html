Your rights

The law is on the borrower's side

The RBI Fair Practices Code, consumer protection law and the courts all limit how a lender may collect, charge and pressure. We use them for you — every case is assessed on its own facts and no outcome is promised.

  • ✓ No lender or recovery agent may threaten you, abuse you, or call your family and workplace to shame you.
  • ✓ Every charge, penalty and interest rate must be disclosed and justified in writing.
  • ✓ You have the right to a fair, documented settlement — not a coerced one.
  • ✓ Collection calls are restricted to reasonable hours under the RBI Fair Practices Code.
